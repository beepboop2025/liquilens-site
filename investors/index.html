The problem

Reviews cross boundaries.
The evidence rarely does.

A bank filing, a funding chart and a market-depth estimate describe different parts of the same exposure. Teams spend time finding the records, reconciling dates and explaining what the data can support. An AI summary makes this harder when its sources and assumptions disappear.

LiquiLens connects those questions in a review workflow, with the underlying records and missing evidence visible. Reviewers can inspect a result, carry it into their tools, and return to its sources.

Global opportunity

Built in India.
Designed to travel.

Treasury, credit and investment teams review exposures across institutions and markets. Financial software and AI agents need those same records in a structured, inspectable form. Websites, REST APIs and hosted MCP give the product system multiple routes into that work.

Our starting point is evidence we already serve: India and US institution research, a deep USD funding desk, broader reference-market coverage and cross-market liquidity research. Expansion follows buyer demand, source rights and jurisdiction-specific validation.

International access is available today. Uniform coverage of every country, security or live quote is an expansion ambition.

Why the products belong together

A useful entry point. A wider review.

One buyer workflow

An institution review can open a funding question; a funding review can expose a market-liquidity question. Teams can start with one product and add the relevant context.

Reusable evidence infrastructure

Source clocks, coverage states, public contracts and review exports make the products easier to inspect and integrate. Reliability and rights remain product-specific obligations.

Human and machine distribution

The same research is available in a browser and through APIs and MCP. Agent builders can integrate existing tools instead of reconstructing the evidence from prose.

Cross-product expansion and reduced integration cost are commercial hypotheses to test, not claimed customer or revenue outcomes.
