Company brief / 5 October 2026

Financial evidence.
Across the whole exposure.

One company. Three connected products. Source-checkable financial research for people, software and AI agents.

The problem

Bank filings, funding charts and market-depth estimates describe different parts of the same exposure. Financial teams and AI agents need a review that connects those records while retaining their dates, definitions and limits.

LIQUILENS

Institution evidence

What is changing at this bank or lender?

Public disclosures, asset quality, capital and funding structure.

SEICHE

Funding conditions

What funding environment surrounds it?

Money markets, reserves, repo and wider source-dated context.

UNDERTOW

Market liquidity

What does liquidity look like at your size?

Market depth, liquidity conditions and stated-size exit estimates.

One connected review

Resolve an institution in LiquiLens. Add relevant funding context from Seiche. Inspect Undertow liquidity evidence when the exposure has a market exit. Export a source-separated review through the browser or an agent workflow.

Each product works independently. Scores and source clocks remain separate; unavailable evidence stays visible.

Built in India. Designed to travel.

International treasury, credit, fund research and financial-software users can access public websites, REST APIs and hosted MCP. Existing coverage includes India and US institution research, European named case files, a deep USD funding desk, broader market references and cross-market liquidity research.

Worldwide access is distinct from market coverage. New jurisdictions require source rights, local definitions and validation.

Commercial direction & diligence

Public entry. Paid recurring work.

LiquiLens: named-list institution-monitoring software and separately scoped paid proof pilots.

Undertow: published Alerts, Agent and Desk subscriptions, a fund offer and machine-priced data.

Seiche: free AGPL public core. Support and custom integration are expansion opportunities that preserve the licence.

Early commercial stage. Published offers are not reported sales. Customers, revenue, retention and pipeline require separate dated diligence evidence.

Why the products belong together

Institution, funding and liquidity questions can arise in the same review. Shared evidence conventions and browser/API/MCP access create reusable integration paths. Repeat use, cross-product adoption and renewal are the next commercial proof.

What capital should unlock

  1. Recurring workflow: prove activation, repeated reviews, paid conversion and renewals for a focused monitoring use case.
  2. Evidence and operations: strengthen admitted sources, freshness, reliability and recovery in markets buyers use.
  3. Demand-led expansion: add integrations and jurisdictions after source-rights and validation review.
  4. Measurement: maintain attributable acquisition, activation, retention and revenue reporting.
