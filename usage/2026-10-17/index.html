What remains unmeasured

Website sessions, attributable external users, returning users, retention, verified paid subscriptions, settled payments and revenue are unavailable. These values remain null in the aggregate, rather than zero. Overall coverage_complete is false.

Application and transport figures are publisher aggregates from the stated logging contracts. Registry figures come from the linked providers. No raw IP addresses, keys, caller identities or request contents are included. This fixed report is historical evidence; it is not a live status monitor.
