Is this a research retainer?

No. You are buying software access against a named list. If most of the fee ever goes to handwritten letters, the letter SKU should be killed. The monthly object is generated from the same packets the public API already serves.

Does LiquiLens rate these names?

No. The board publishes screens, not credit ratings, and not a claim that a living institution will fail. Historical diagnostics keep both eligibility flags false on the public record.

What if a name is not on the live board?

The pack marks it uncovered or stale. Absence is not green. We do not invent a score from a similar name. A European legal name that is not on the India or US board stays uncovered. Europe is seven named case files, not a monthly named-list book.

Has this been tested on US and other institutions?

Yes, as public historical diagnostics with the eligibility flags kept false. India is a construction-PIT record over 48 institutions. The United States is a current-amended construction-PIT record over 552 FDIC failures since 2008 (72.8 percent recall on the served validation). Europe is seven named case files, not a cohort statistic. These are not a validated backtest and not real-money eligible. The named-list seat copies the live boards; it does not promote those diagnostics into a rating.

Where do Seiche and Undertow sit?

Same lab, different jobs. Seiche is free dollar-funding plumbing. Undertow is exit cost. The named-list invoice is LiquiLens. The morning card on Telegram joins the three reads; it does not sell the other two.
