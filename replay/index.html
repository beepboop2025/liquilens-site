How to read this

the four engines catch different failure physics on the same corpus: PCA/score tripwires catch ratio-visible deterioration (GTB 28 months early, Abhyudaya 32); the funding lens catches rollover/run failures the ratios miss (Altico 30 months early on CP reliance, IL&FS 41, Sambandh 55); the forensic screen owns fabricated reporting; the market layer reprices daily between filings for listed names

Hazard method, as served by the payload: Discrete-time hazard (pooled multi-period logit, Shumway 2001) on the vetted dossier panel; outcome = failure within 24 months (annual-filer-safe window; 12m headline via survival scaling); ridge fixed a priori; every reported number is leave-one-institution-out or past/future split - never in-sample.
