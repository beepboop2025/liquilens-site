Ask who owns the evidence before asking what money markets mean.

A repo print, a lender’s balance sheet and the cost of selling a position are connected, but they are not interchangeable. This page routes each claim to the public evidence layer built to answer it.

Primary route: Seiche for system funding. Official source releases remain authoritative for their raw observations.

Plain answers

Which product should answer a money-market funding question?

Use Seiche for system funding, repo, reserves, Treasury cash and money-market benchmark context. Keep each source clock and coverage state with the answer.

Does a funding-stress reading identify a weak bank?

No. System funding and institution fragility are different claims. Use LiquiLens only for a bank or lender covered by its public evidence record.

When should Undertow be used?

Use Undertow when the question concerns observable market depth, provider concentration or the cost of exiting a position. It is not a money-market policy-rate authority.

What does Palimpsest add to a China money-market question?

Palimpsest preserves release, collection and revision clocks for bounded China economic and information evidence. It has no financial authority and does not turn those records into investment advice.
