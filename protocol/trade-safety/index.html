Control path

Discover with agents. Enforce at the order boundary.

MCP, FDC3 and natural-language explanations make the control easy to discover. The hard guarantee comes from architecture: the agent receives only a wrapped order action, while raw broker credentials and direct submit tools remain outside its inventory.

1 · Proposed orderBind agent, operator, tenant, account, instrument, side, size, prices and policy.
2 · Product evidenceFetch request-bound Seiche and Undertow sections; attach eligible LiquiLens context when applicable, otherwise emit the required not_applicable section.
3 · Policy checkValidate clocks, rights, source identity and operator-authored thresholds deterministically.
4 · ReceiptIssue a short-lived identity with all-false financial authority and an explicit outcome.
5 · VerifyThe caller or OMS verifies the exact bytes before its own reject, review, resubmit or submit action.
Outcomes

No hidden score, no silent downgrade.

Pass

This exact request satisfied this exact operator policy at the receipt clock. It is not trade approval or advice.

Limit

An explicit size, exit-cost or spread constraint was exceeded. Build and re-evaluate a new order; never mutate the old one.

Hold

A valid observed condition matched an operator hold rule. It is not a market prediction.

Unavailable

Required evidence, rights, freshness, authentication or live prerequisites failed. It is not zero, calm, neutral or bearish.

Evidence roles

Keep each product native.

ProductRoleBaselineBoundary

The protocol never averages product states or clocks into a universal risk score. Missing, stale, restricted, unsupported and unavailable evidence remain explicit and fail closed when required.
