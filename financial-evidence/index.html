Financial Evidence Research Review

Bring source-linked funding and institution research into Google Sheets, with dates, missing values and coverage gaps kept beside the evidence.

Google Sheets Editor add-on. Choose up to five currencies and five exact institution slugs. Each explicit refresh creates new review tabs in your current spreadsheet and lets you download the original public evidence JSON.

The Google Marketplace add-on is undergoing OAuth and Marketplace review. Public installation is not yet available. The browser research desk is available separately.

Open the browser research desk

What the review contains

  • Published funding observations with currency-specific units and source dates.
  • Covered-institution research, with unknown institutions clearly marked as not covered.
  • Gaps and request status, including unavailable, missing and withheld values.
  • A new capture on every refresh, preserving your existing spreadsheet analysis.

Your spreadsheet and permissions

The add-on works only in the spreadsheet where you invoke it. It does not read existing cell values or upload workbook contents. Only the public currency codes and institution slugs you enter are sent to the research service. No background refresh runs.

The runtime uses current-spreadsheet, sidebar and external-request permissions. Marketplace installation also lists email-address and basic-profile permissions; the code does not read or transmit those identity fields. Read the privacy policy for Google data access, use, sharing, protection and deletion details.

Research for human review

Seiche supplies funding evidence and LiquiLens supplies institution evidence. Coverage can be partial, delayed or unavailable. A new retrieval time does not make an older observation current. Missing or withheld numbers remain blank, never zero.

This is research software, not financial advice, a credit rating, a deposit-safety guarantee, a trading signal or trade execution. Public research is free of charge and requires no Financial Evidence account or API key. Google account permissions and quotas still apply.
