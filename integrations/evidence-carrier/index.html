Integration · portable provenance

Move the evidence without dropping what makes it evidence.

The Apache-2.0 Evidence Carrier contract preserves product identity, event and knowledge clocks, hashes, source rights, revision history and an all-false financial-authority boundary across files, agents and data systems.

The integration sequence

Receive

Accept a full Carrier or policy-redacted reference only from a producer that can truthfully provide the required identity, clocks and rights.

Verify

Use the released verifier to validate the schema, content address, expiry, rights disposition and all-false authority boundary.

Reject

Unknown or restricted rights, invalid hashes, expired evidence or missing clocks remain rejected or metadata-only. Do not repair them with guesses.

Current ecosystem boundary

The four live product APIs do not yet publish native Carrier endpoints. Financial Evidence therefore retrieves raw JSON and reports carrier verification as not performed. A downstream integration must not mint provenance on their behalf.

Questions from data teams

Can an integration create a Carrier from any JSON response?

No. A valid Carrier needs truthful producer identity, clocks, provenance and redistribution rights. A downstream reader must not invent those fields.

Does a valid Carrier authorise trading or credit decisions?

No. Execution, recommendation, financial and credit-rating authority remain explicitly false.
