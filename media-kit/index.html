LiquiLens is a public-evidence early-warning system for banks and lenders, with explicit misses, coverage and claim boundaries.

Directory description

LiquiLens helps risk, treasury and research teams investigate covered banks, NBFCs, MFIs and lenders using public filings, funding structure, regulatory thresholds and market signals. Its free Financial Evidence Desk also routes bounded public evidence across LiquiLens, Seiche, Undertow and Palimpsest. Outputs are research screens—not credit ratings, investment advice or predictions that an institution will fail.
