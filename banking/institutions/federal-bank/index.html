What this record does not establish

  • A falling NPA percentage does not identify recoveries; loan growth, write-offs and sales can also change it.
  • GNPA and NNPA use different denominators. Their ratio or difference is not provision coverage.
  • A zero reported NNPA is not proof of zero credit risk or full deposit safety.
  • Institution type is the dossier's reporting classification; licence transitions require separate verification.
  • Selected facts verified against the cited primary filing; capture time is not backdated to publication.
  • Primary filing re-reviewed on 5 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata) from the bank-hosted document. The June 2026 reporting period is unchanged; earlier knowledge versions and their source identities are retained.

This page assigns no credit score, bank ranking, regulatory action or deposit-safety verdict. Supervisory scope and licence changes require separate dated evidence.
