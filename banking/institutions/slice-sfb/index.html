What this record does not establish

  • A falling NPA percentage does not identify recoveries; loan growth, write-offs and sales can also change it.
  • GNPA and NNPA use different denominators. Their ratio or difference is not provision coverage.
  • A zero reported NNPA is not proof of zero credit risk or full deposit safety.
  • Institution type is the dossier's reporting classification; licence transitions require separate verification.
  • Amounts in the movement table are rounded to INR thousands. Component reductions and reported totals have small rounding differences; those residuals are retained.
