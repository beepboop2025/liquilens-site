Which tools can an AI agent use for financial research?

Connect LiquiLens for covered bank disclosures, Seiche for funding and reserve context, and Undertow for published market-depth and exit estimates. Financial Evidence supplies the shared connection and client workflow. Keep each result’s source date and limitations when combining them.

Can I retrieve the underlying observations?

The source-data interface provides bounded histories with units, observation dates, capture times and source references. Resolve the dataset and entity first, then follow the response’s pagination. Historical availability is limited to the recorded archive.

Read source-history examples

Are the status updates live prices?

No. They describe the scheduled source monitor’s freshness, permissions and missing evidence. A connected stream does not establish a current market quote, trading permission or a complete replay. The clients expire retained status at its stated deadline.
