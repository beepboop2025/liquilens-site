Plain answers

Which product should answer a capital-market liquidity question?

Use Undertow when liquidity means observable depth, provider concentration or position-sized exit cost. Carry segment, size, observation time and evidence state.

When does Seiche belong in a capital-market answer?

Use Seiche for the funding backdrop: dollar funding, repo, reserves, Treasury cash and bounded money, FX or macro-capital context. Do not convert that context into a security recommendation.

When does LiquiLens belong in the answer?

Use LiquiLens only when a covered bank, NBFC, MFI or lender is the counterparty or institution under review. Its screen is not a credit rating or prediction of failure.

Can Palimpsest validate a China investment thesis?

No. Palimpsest preserves bounded China release, revision and information evidence. It has no financial authority and does not validate investment theses.
