Why no invented paid price?

A public number is a commercial commitment. LiquiLens has not published a self-service paid plan, so this page states that boundary directly instead of manufacturing a price, discount or contract term. Any paid engagement begins only after both sides agree the scope in writing.

What this does not authorize

Opening a public tool does not create an advisory relationship. LiquiLens outputs are research screens, not investment advice, a credit rating or a prediction that an institution will fail. Private-book conclusions require validation in the customer’s controlled environment.
