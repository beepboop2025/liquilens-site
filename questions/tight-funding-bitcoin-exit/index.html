If funding is tight, can a large bitcoin position still get out?

Only if sufficient executable liquidity is available at the relevant venue, size and time, subject to account and settlement constraints. A dollar-funding signal cannot settle that question. In the retained evidence, Undertow estimates a $1 million BTC sell at 2.081 bp on Binance on 6 October 2026, while Seiche’s separate 2 October SOFR–IORB observation is −2 bp. These do not establish an actual fill or a tight-funding event.

What must be checked before claiming exit capacity?

Specify the asset, sell side, USD notional, venue, quote currency and observation time. Check that the requested size has a published estimate and that the depth and conversion evidence cover it. A missing venue is not a venue with zero cost.

Undertow’s estimates are band-interpolated research outputs. An executable exit also depends on the actual book when the order arrives, your access, order constraints and settlement. A displayed estimate is not an execution acknowledgement.

How the products divide the work

Use Seiche to inspect funding conditions. Use Undertow to inspect the size-specific market-depth evidence. Use LiquiLens when a separate institution or counterparty-disclosure question arises. A bank NPA ratio is not an exchange solvency score.

Tighter funding and a liquid BTC book can coexist, just as an easy funding environment can coexist with a thin book on a particular venue. Establishing either relationship for an episode requires synchronized evidence; the retained dates here do not provide it.

What this evidence cannot establish

  • These are dated, band-interpolated single-venue sell estimates. They are not executable quotes or a promise that an order will fill.
  • The estimate does not establish an all-in trading cost including your fee tier, latency, subsequent order-book changes or settlement constraints.
  • USD and USDT books require explicit conversion. USDT is not silently treated as one US dollar.
  • The venue sample is limited. A low estimate does not establish venue safety, account eligibility or the best route across all exchanges.
  • Public publication of a derived estimate does not establish independent redistribution rights for the underlying venue data.
  • No synchronized funding-to-BTC stress relationship is established by the retained examples.
