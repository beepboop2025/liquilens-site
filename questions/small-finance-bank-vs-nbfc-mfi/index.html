India bank and NBFC risk / evidence explained

What is the difference between a small finance bank and an NBFC-MFI?

A small finance bank is a bank that can accept deposits and lend; an NBFC-MFI is a non-bank microfinance lender in a non-deposit-taking category. That distinction changes the funding evidence a researcher should inspect. For example, AU Small Finance Bank’s accepted 30 June 2026 filing reports ₹157,727 crore of deposits and ₹140,461 crore of gross advances. Those are bank balance-sheet measures, not a template for an NBFC-MFI’s funding mix.

Different funding questions

For a deposit-taking bank, inspect deposit concentration, withdrawal sensitivity, maturity, liquid assets and capital alongside loan quality. The absolute deposit total does not reveal concentration or how quickly funding could leave.

For an NBFC-MFI, inspect borrowings, maturity mismatches, bank and market-funding concentration, refinancing terms and liquidity buffers. “Microfinance” describes an activity or regulatory category; it does not make every institution’s balance sheet interchangeable.

How to avoid a misleading comparison

Use the current legal entity and reporting period. Do not carry an old NBFC label through a banking-licence transition without checking it. LiquiLens retains dossier classifications and explicitly notes where separate licensing verification is needed.

Compare ratios only when their numerator, denominator, accounting scope and observation period agree. A missing deposit metric at a non-deposit-taking lender is not a zero-risk signal, and a bank’s deposit base is not a repayment guarantee.

What this evidence cannot establish

  • This is a selected set of accepted public filings, not a census of Indian lenders or a ranking of deposit safety.
  • A change in an NPA ratio is measured in percentage points. Recoveries, loan growth, write-offs and sales can all affect ratios; the ratio alone does not identify the cause.
  • The current model card does not establish an out-of-time validated false-positive rate. Historical replay evidence is not a validated probability of failure.
  • A June reporting period remains June evidence even when it is retrieved or reviewed in October. A newer unreviewed filing may exist.
