India bank and NBFC risk / evidence explained

Which small finance banks have rising NPAs?

In LiquiLens’s seven covered small finance banks with comparable June 2026 quarter-end records, AU Small Finance Bank was the only bank with a higher gross NPA ratio than in March: 2.10%, up 0.07 percentage point. Two ratios were unchanged and four fell. This answers a narrow question about those accepted filings; it does not say AU is unsafe or that every other small finance bank improved.

Higher than last quarter is different from a high level

Suryoday’s retained GNPA ratio is 6.50%, Utkarsh’s 5.90% and ESAF’s 5.40%. A high level and a rising level are different questions: Suryoday and ESAF were unchanged quarter over quarter in this accepted set, while Utkarsh fell by 1.70 percentage points.

AU’s 0.07 percentage-point quarterly increase also coexists with a 0.37 percentage-point year-on-year decline. State the comparison period before describing an institution as deteriorating.

What should be checked in the underlying filing?

Read gross and net NPA ratios alongside the loan denominator, new slippages, write-offs, recoveries and provisioning basis. Then inspect capital, funding concentration and maturity. A falling ratio can reflect a larger loan book or a write-off rather than improved borrower cash flows.

Where an accepted record does not contain a closed NPA movement table, LiquiLens marks that gap. Do not manufacture a recovery rate from the difference between GNPA and NNPA. Those ratios use different denominators.

What this evidence cannot establish

  • This is a selected set of accepted public filings, not a census of Indian lenders or a ranking of deposit safety.
  • A change in an NPA ratio is measured in percentage points. Recoveries, loan growth, write-offs and sales can all affect ratios; the ratio alone does not identify the cause.
  • The current model card does not establish an out-of-time validated false-positive rate. Historical replay evidence is not a validated probability of failure.
  • A June reporting period remains June evidence even when it is retrieved or reviewed in October. A newer unreviewed filing may exist.
