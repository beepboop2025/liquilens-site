The agent sequence

1. Route the claim

Choose the evidence domain explicitly. Do not let free-form text silently blend institution risk with system funding or market exits.

2. Retrieve bounded JSON

The public tool follows fixed HTTPS routes, rejects redirects, bounds bytes and preserves raw product separation.

3. Cite the boundary

Report retrieval, source-reported state, clocks and carrier verification independently. Missing evidence stays unavailable.

Decision boundary

The public interfaces are read-only research infrastructure. They grant no execution, recommendation, credit-rating or financial authority. An agent must not infer healthy evidence from HTTP success, missing fields or an empty source.

Questions builders ask

Does the public Financial Evidence endpoint require authentication?

No. The hosted read-only endpoint and fixed public routes require no token.

Does a successful tool call prove evidence completeness?

No. Transport status is separate from evidence evaluation and Evidence Carrier verification.
