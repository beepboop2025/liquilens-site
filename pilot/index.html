Fit

This pilot is useful when three things are true.

A real owner

A risk, credit, finance or treasury leader owns both the current workflow and the go/no-go decision.

A measurable history

The team can provide a controlled historical extract with outcomes, timestamps and the existing baseline.

A deployable boundary

There is a sandbox or supervised environment where the code can run without copying the book to LiquiLens.

Why is the pilot paid?

It includes deployment, a frozen study design, an honest comparison and a filable governance pack. The fee keeps the work bounded and is credited in full if the product earns a production contract.

Does LiquiLens make credit decisions during the pilot?

No. This is a parallel screen. It does not approve, reject, downgrade, classify or execute. A named human retains every decision.

Does our book leave our environment?

Self-hosted is the default. The first scoping call requires no customer rows, and the pilot agreement defines the exact data boundary before access.

What if the agreed bar is missed?

The pilot stops at week six. You keep the result, including the misses and the reason the bar was not met. There is no production obligation.
