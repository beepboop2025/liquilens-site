## Why the warning mattered

A funding warning matters because liabilities can require continuing confidence and refinancing while assets may not convert into cash on matching terms. A disclosed liability pattern can therefore merit scrutiny before a formal action-zone measure appears in a public record.

For IL&FS, the defensible conclusion is limited: the funding lens identified its earliest designated disclosed stress evidence at the 2015-03-31 period end, using the 2015-05-30 proxy clock. It was earlier than any recorded PCA action-zone entry because the record contains none. That does not prove the funding signal caused the subsequent default or that it was sufficient to diagnose the institution.

Fraud masking is decisive to interpretation. The PCA summary states that filings later shown falsified can look compliant to a threshold engine, and that the forensic screen owns those cases. A compliant-looking disclosed ratio is not proof of a sound underlying balance sheet. Equally, a visible funding warning can be partial, distorted, or incidental to facts the disclosed series could not show.

## The strongest counter-case

The strongest counter-case can defeat the thesis. The apparent 38-month lead may largely be a construction artefact. A period-end plus 60-day proxy is not a complete historical information set, and the dossier explicitly calls lead times optimistic. A full bitemporal archive could shift the availability date, alter the observed sequence, or remove the apparent advantage.

Coverage is a second challenge. Only 10 of 15 failed institutions had usable liability disclosures. A lens that excludes institutions without that series cannot establish that funding risk ranks reliably across all institutions. Missing data must remain missing; it cannot be recoded as a healthy balance sheet.

Third, IL&FS is fraud-masked. The screen may have detected a real disclosed vulnerability, but it cannot establish whether that vulnerability was the dominant mechanism, nor reconstruct unavailable facts. The missing PCA lead is likewise not evidence that PCA-type conditions were absent. The thesis survives only as a prompt to examine disclosed funding, not as a model victory.
