The warning mattered because it described a concrete liability-side exposure before the default date. Commercial paper must be refinanced. But plausibility is not proof of a complete causal history. The evidence does not provide a contemporaneous Altico market-price series, an observed rollover refusal, or a dated PCA breach. The responsible reading is that a stable composite band coexisted with a specific funding flag, and that the flag was the first recorded signal in the replay.

## The strongest counter-case

The counter-case can defeat any stronger thesis. Commercial-paper reliance of **29%** does not itself demonstrate imminent failure. Altico’s funding index was **23.7** and classified as **stable**. The public record contains no first PCA action-zone date, and no Altico market distance-to-default. A lender may retain access to refinancing, change its funding mix, or avoid the feared outcome. On this evidence alone, a funding flag is not a verdict.

The counter-case leaves a smaller claim standing: Altico’s disclosed funding structure identified a plausible first stress channel well before default, not a proven prediction.
