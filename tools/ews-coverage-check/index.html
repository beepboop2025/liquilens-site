How to read the result

A coverage score is not a model-validation result.

The checklist asks whether twelve controls exist and can be demonstrated. It does not test predictive performance, data quality, calibration, regulatory compliance or the risk of any institution. Use the gap report to structure a review, then verify every signal against its source and local policy.

Does this score a bank?

No. No institution data enters the page. The number describes checklist coverage only and is not a credit rating or prediction of failure.

Do answers leave the browser?

No. Checkbox answers and report text stay local. Only aggregate events such as “report copied” may be counted, without properties or personal data.
