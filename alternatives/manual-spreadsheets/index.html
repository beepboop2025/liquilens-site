A spreadsheet records the answer. LiquiLens records what the answer knew.

A practical migration

Keep

Exposure, limits, analyst judgment, approvals and customer-confidential data in your controlled workflow.

Automate

Public-source collection, institution coverage checks, factor refreshes and a consistent evidence packet.

Do not infer

An empty cell, unavailable name or successful API response is not a low-risk signal.

Decision boundary

LiquiLens does not replace a credit policy, rating, exposure system or accountable human review. It is a public-evidence layer that can make the spreadsheet’s inputs easier to inspect.

Questions before migrating

Does LiquiLens replace a credit analyst's spreadsheet?

No. It can replace repetitive public-evidence collection and status tracking, while the analyst retains exposure context, judgment and decision ownership.

Can a team export the evidence?

Yes. Public JSON routes and the Evidence Desk expose downloadable evidence, subject to each source's redistribution rights and boundaries.
