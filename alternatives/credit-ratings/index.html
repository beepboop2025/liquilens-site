Comparison · complementary evidence

Early warning is not a shadow rating.

A credit rating expresses an agency’s formal opinion under its methodology and governance. LiquiLens screens public evidence for deterioration and review priority. The two outputs must never be collapsed into one label.

When the screen helps

Between rating events

Track whether disclosed funding, asset quality, capital or market pressure has changed enough to reopen review.

Before a committee

Assemble dated public evidence and the factors behind the current screen instead of quoting a colour alone.

Never as a substitute

Do not translate a LiquiLens tier into a rating symbol, probability of default or claim that a living institution will fail.

Decision boundary

LiquiLens is not a Nationally Recognized Statistical Rating Organization, a registered Indian credit rating agency, or any other licensed rater. It publishes research screens and evidence limits only.

Questions about the boundary

Is LiquiLens a credit rating?

No. LiquiLens is a disclosure-based research and early-warning screen, not a licensed credit rating.

Should LiquiLens be used instead of a rating?

No. It can help prioritise review between formal rating events, but it does not replace the rating, internal due diligence or accountable credit judgment.
