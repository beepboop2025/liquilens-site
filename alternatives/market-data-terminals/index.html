Where LiquiLens is deliberately smaller

Bounded routes

Each claim domain has an explicit product. The system does not pretend to be a universal market database.

Public evidence

The open surfaces use public, redistributable or link-only material according to source policy.

No execution authority

There is no trading, portfolio recommendation, rating or substitute for licensed source data.

Decision boundary

Do not copy licensed terminal data into a public Evidence Carrier unless the source rights allow it. An open protocol never widens the licence attached to the underlying evidence.

Questions research teams ask

Does LiquiLens replace a market-data terminal?

No. A terminal offers broad licensed data and workflows. LiquiLens offers a narrower public-evidence early-warning and provenance layer.

Can the two be used together?

Yes. A team can use its licensed terminal for broad market data and LiquiLens for bounded public-evidence screens, source status and reproducible research packets.
