THE ORIGINALITY GATE

What has to be new

Public facts are inputs. The publishable unit is the desk’s own reproducible analytical contribution.

01

Longitudinal delta

A measured change against our own point-in-time history—not a standing level dressed up as news.

02

Evidence braid

Independent records that strengthen, contradict or bound one another, with the relationship named.

03

Revision or absence

A source changed, disappeared, arrived late or left a denominator-shaped hole worth recording.

04

Forward adjudication

A call sealed before its outcome becomes judgeable; hits, misses and voids remain on the record.
