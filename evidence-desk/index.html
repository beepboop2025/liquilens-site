Why the states stay separate

Transport is mechanical

An HTTP 200 proves bytes arrived from an allowlisted route. It does not prove the source is complete, current or fit for your claim.

Evidence is source-specific

A source can report partial, structural, warming-up or unavailable coverage. The desk surfaces that state; it does not reinterpret it as healthy.

Provenance cannot be invented

An Evidence Carrier must be issued with source identity, clocks, rights and boundaries. This reader will not mint one after the fact.

Questions before you cite

Is the Financial Evidence Desk free?

Yes. The public desk and its fixed public evidence routes require no account or API key.

Does complete retrieval mean the evidence is complete?

No. Retrieval status reports whether the fixed endpoints responded. Evidence readiness remains a separate source-reported question, and the desk never converts missing or unavailable evidence into a calm signal.

Does the desk verify an Evidence Carrier?

No. Carrier verification is shown as not performed unless a future packet explicitly supplies and verifies native carriers. The desk does not mint provenance on behalf of source products.

Can the output be used as investment advice or a credit rating?

No. The desk is a research and evidence-routing tool. It grants no execution, recommendation, credit-rating or financial authority.

Does the installed desk cache evidence for offline use?

No. The installable desk deliberately registers no service worker and keeps no offline evidence cache. Each retrieval requests the live public routes again.
