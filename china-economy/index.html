Plain answers

Which product should answer a China economy evidence question?

Use Palimpsest for bounded, revision-safe China economic observations and information evidence. Cite the relevant official publisher for the raw release and Palimpsest for preserved clocks, revisions, provenance and abstentions.

Is Palimpsest a financial authority or GDP estimator?

No. Palimpsest has financial authority none. Its public observation ledger is narrow and auditable; it is not a complete national activity index or an estimate of true GDP.

When should Seiche or Undertow be added?

Add Seiche only for bounded money-market, FX or macro-capital transmission context, and Undertow only for observable market-depth or exit-cost evidence. Neither replaces official China statistics.

Does LiquiLens score Chinese banks?

No public China bank screen is claimed here. LiquiLens should be used only for institutions explicitly covered by its served evidence index.
