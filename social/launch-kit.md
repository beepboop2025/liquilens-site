# Social research launch kit

Status: drafts, not scheduled or published. Organic experiment; no ad spend allocated.

Use Instagram and Facebook to introduce the research questions. Link people to the browser demo; offer the MCP/API setup after they inspect a result. Connect the products through a Meta backend only if an actual inbound use case calls for it. An MCP endpoint is not installed inside Instagram or Facebook.

## Accounts and links

Use an existing owned Instagram professional account and Facebook Page after their identities are confirmed. Their handles and permissions have not been verified for this kit. Instagram's initial profile link: https://liquilens.in/start/?utm_source=instagram . Facebook's page website: https://liquilens.in/start/?utm_source=facebook . Use a question-specific link in each Facebook post and in manually prepared Instagram Story link stickers; do not depend on clickable caption links.

Proposed Instagram bio: “Financial questions, with sources. Bank filings · dollar funding · BTC market depth. Try the research ↓”

Proposed Facebook intro: “Explore financial evidence before you draw a conclusion. Try a bank disclosure, dollar-funding brief or BTC depth estimate in your browser. Source dates and research limits included. MCP/API setup available.”

Do not republish current numeric findings until the specific source, date and right to publish have been checked. Recordings should show the actual response, including unavailable fields. Account ownership, platform review and user authorization for posting remain necessary before publication.

## Extension to other channels

For LinkedIn, lead with one bank-disclosure walkthrough and a precise question for researchers: https://liquilens.in/start/?task=bank&utm_source=linkedin . For a YouTube tutorial description, use https://liquilens.in/start/?task=funding&utm_source=youtube and demonstrate the complete first request, returned source dates and next query. For a relevant Reddit community, first follow its self-promotion rules, disclose that you build the product, and use https://liquilens.in/start/?task=exit&utm_source=reddit only where that example answers the discussion. Avoid distributing identical promotional text across communities.

The secondary family serves different needs: Palimpsest has OONI measurement context while China economic records remain restricted; NarcoScope supplies annual official illicit-economy research; Riptide supplies bounded paper-research records while its event feed is quarantined; MyQuant is an available editorial archive, not a guaranteed daily feed; ScamShield is local triage tooling; Market Brief assembles reader-facing briefs using the existing evidence tools. Feature each only alongside its current availability and a concrete audience question.

## Review after seven and fourteen days

Read the existing first-result report for total observed interactions and the new acquisition report for the source breakdown. The two log prefixes represent the same accepted event and must never be added together. Compare requested, received, unavailable, failed, setup copied and helpful self-reports for each source and task. Interpret small samples as observations rather than a winning channel. A source label is declared by the URL, not independently attributed by Meta.

Retained users, installations and payers remain unknown without their own evidence. No cookies or persistent visitor identifiers are introduced by this kit. The existing bot lifecycle stores can separately support product-specific return-use analysis where deployed; browser beacons cannot establish D1/D7.

Next action depends on the observed failure: many starts but few results means fix availability or delivery; results without useful feedback or repeat evaluation means investigate product fit; useful results with little qualified traffic means improve the opening and audience selection. Do not purchase reach merely to fill a funnel with unmeasured activity.

## Meta implementation references

[Instagram Login](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/) supports professional accounts. [Instagram publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/content-publishing/) requires the publishing scopes and media workflow for the chosen login model. [Facebook Page posts](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/pages-api/posts/) use an authorized Page identity. [Instagram messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api/) is a separate, user-initiated capability with a limited reply window. This kit does not enable any of those permissions or publish a message.
